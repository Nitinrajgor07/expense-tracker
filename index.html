Privacy Policy

Your financial data is 100% private and stays offline

1. Offline Storage: This application stores all transaction details, budgets, and settings locally on your device's browser database (localStorage). No financial data is ever transmitted to external servers.

2. No Registration Required: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details (such as names or email addresses) to use this expense tracker.

3. Third-Party Access: We do not track, sell, or share your usage metrics. There are no external tracking SDKs, advertisers, or cloud synchronizers attached to this software.

4. Local Backups: The automated backup files are saved directly in your device's offline memory. If you delete your browser data or clear storage, your offline records will be erased unless you download a manual JSON backup.
